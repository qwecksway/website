Our Story

From a continuation school to a technical education landmark

Founded in 1987 as a Community Secondary Technical School on the site of the former Continuation School, AFASTECH began with just 6 staff and 80 students. It remained a day school until Free SHS brought boarding status in September 2017. Today, under the leadership of Headmistress Ophylia Kumah — the school's 12th head since founding head Mr. W. K. Ameko — AFASTECH serves around 300 students across five programmes.
