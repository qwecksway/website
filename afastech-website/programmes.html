Our programmes

Five pathways, each pairing core academics with practical, hands-on skill.

Agricultural Science Business General Arts Home Economics Technical Skills
Programme 1

Agricultural Science

Grounded in the land AFASTECH sits on at the foot of Mount Afadja, this programme builds scientific understanding of crop and animal production alongside the maths needed for agribusiness and further study.

Core subjects

  • Animal Husbandry
  • General Agriculture
  • Chemistry
  • Elective Mathematics
Programme 2

Business

A foundation in accounting, management and economics for students headed toward commerce, finance, or entrepreneurship — including running a small enterprise of their own.

Core subjects

  • Business Management
  • Principles of Cost Accounting
  • Financial Accounting
  • Economics
  • Elective Mathematics
  • Computing
Programme 3

General Arts

A humanities programme spanning history, government, language and literature, giving students a broad grounding for law, education, media and public service careers.

Core subjects

  • Economics
  • Geography
  • Elective Mathematics
  • Government
  • History
  • French
  • Ghanaian Language (Ewe)
  • Literature
Programme 4

Home Economics

Combining science, nutrition and design, this programme prepares students for careers in food science, textiles, hospitality and family and consumer sciences.

Core subjects

  • Biology
  • Foods and Nutrition
  • Clothing and Textiles
  • General Knowledge in Arts
  • Management in Living
  • Arts Design Foundation
Programme 5

Technical Skills / Applied Technology

AFASTECH's namesake programme — the one the tape measure on our crest speaks to directly. Students build real, hands-on capability in woodwork, electricals, construction and technical drawing.

Core subjects

  • Woodwork
  • Technical Drawing
  • Elective Mathematics
  • Physics
  • Applied Electricity
  • Building Construction
  • Design Communication Technology
  • Electricals & Electronic Technology
  • Computing
