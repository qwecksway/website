About Afadjato Senior High Technical School

Knowledge, Integrity, Patriotism — since 1987.

Our History

A community's commitment to disciplined learning

Afadjato Senior High Technical School was founded in 1987 as a Community Secondary Technical School, occupying the former Continuation School to educate students of the Gbledi community and its environs. The school sits on approximately 10.33 acres of land, just 300 metres from the foot of Mount Afadja — the highest peak in Ghana and West Africa.

It began as a day school with a founding population of 6 staff and 80 students, and attained boarding status at the start of the Free SHS programme in September 2017. The school has since been led by 12 headmasters, beginning with Mr. W. K. Ameko. The current head, Ms. Ophylia Kumah, has been in post since July 2024. Today, AFASTECH serves a population of about 300 students.

The Crest

The school crest carries the motto Knowledge, Integrity, and Patriotism, and an emblem of Mount Afadja rising above an open book and a tape measure. Knowledge denotes individual power; Integrity indicates moral uprightness in honesty and truthfulness; Patriotism speaks to care for public property, communal spirit, and respect for the laws of the land. Mount Afadja signifies the sky as the limit for learners, the open book represents the store of academic knowledge students are exposed to, and the tape measure reminds learners to take accurate measurements in all aspects of their work. The school's green and yellow colours signify the luxuriant evergreen forest around the school, and the wealth held in its soil.
